About Trader Jue

Our Story

Trader Jue is your go-to destination for simple, delicious tropical cocktails. I've always been a fan of tropical cocktails so I decided to create a website to share all the recipes I've collected over the years. If the cocktail is a copycat of a classic, I will try to provide a link to the source or at least the owner of the original recipe.

Whether you're planning a beach getaway, hosting friends, or simply craving a taste of the tropics, our curated collection of cocktail recipes brings the sunshine to your glass.
