Trinidad Sour

History:

The Trinidad Sour turns the usual cocktail upside down by using Angostura bitters as the main ingredient. Bartender Giuseppe Gonzalez created it in 2009 at the Clover Club in Brooklyn, inspired by an earlier competition-winning drink, the Trinidad Especial. Angostura bitters are made in Trinidad, which gives the drink its name.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ oz Angostura bitters
  • ½ oz rye whiskey
  • 1 oz orgeat syrup
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Serve without garnish.
