Strawberry Daiquiri

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz light rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 1 cup frozen strawberries
  • 1 cup ice

Instructions:

  1. In a blender, add all the ingredients except the ice and pulse until smooth.
  2. Add the ice and blend until slushy.
  3. Pour into a chilled glass.
  4. Garnish with a fresh strawberry.
