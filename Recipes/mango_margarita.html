Mango Margarita

History:

This is a frozen, fruity take on the Margarita. The frozen Margarita took off in 1971, when Dallas restaurant owner Mariano Martinez turned a soft-serve ice cream machine into the first frozen margarita machine, which is now in the Smithsonian. Fruit versions like mango soon became favorites.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz triple sec
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz agave nectar
  • 1 cup frozen mango chunks
  • 1 cup ice

Instructions:

  1. In a blender, add all the ingredients except the ice and pulse until smooth.
  2. Add the ice and blend until slushy.
  3. Pour into a salt or chili-lime rimmed glass.
  4. Garnish with a lime wheel.

Source:
