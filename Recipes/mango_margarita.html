Mango Margarita

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz triple sec
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz agave nectar
  • 1 cup frozen mango chunks
  • 1 cup ice

Instructions:

  1. In a blender, add all the ingredients except the ice and pulse until smooth.
  2. Add the ice and blend until slushy.
  3. Pour into a salt or chili-lime rimmed glass.
  4. Garnish with a lime wheel.
