Scorpion

History:

Trader Vic created the Scorpion, around the 1950s. It was famously served in big shared bowls, and Life magazine reported that his restaurants limited each group to three bowls. The recipe here is a single serving, adapted from the one in Trader Vic's 1972 bartender's guide.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz light rum
  • 1 oz brandy
  • 1½ oz fresh orange juice
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz orgeat syrup
  • Crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender with a cup of crushed ice.
  2. Flash blend for 5 seconds.
  3. Pour into a double rocks glass or tiki bowl.
  4. Garnish with a gardenia or mint sprig.

Source:
