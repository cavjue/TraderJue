Guava Spritz

History:

Spritz drinks date back to the 1800s, when soldiers in Italy's Veneto region, then part of the Austrian Empire, added a splash of soda water to the local wine. The name comes from the German word spritzen, “to splash.” This guava version keeps the light, bubbly spirit of a spritz with a tropical twist.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • 2 oz guava juice
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • 4 fresh mint leaves
  • Club soda
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Gently muddle mint and simple syrup in a glass.
  2. Add vodka, guava juice, and lime juice.
  3. Fill with ice and top with club soda.
  4. Stir gently and garnish.
