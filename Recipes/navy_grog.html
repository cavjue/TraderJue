Navy Grog

Ingredients:

  • 1 oz Demerara rum
  • 1 oz Jamaican rum
  • 1 oz white rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz white grapefruit juice
  • 1 oz honey syrup
  • ¾ oz club soda
  • Crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Add the rums, lime juice, grapefruit juice, and honey syrup to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a double rocks glass over crushed ice.
  4. Top with club soda and garnish with a mint sprig.
