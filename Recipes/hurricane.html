Hurricane

History:

The Hurricane is famous as the signature drink of Pat O'Brien's bar in New Orleans, where it was made popular in the 1940s. It may have started at the Hurricane Bar in New York in 1939. One popular story says Pat O'Brien's created its version to use up a surplus of rum. It's named after the curvy hurricane-lamp glass it's served in.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz light rum
  • 2 oz dark rum
  • 2 oz passion fruit juice
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 tbsp simple syrup
  • 1 tbsp grenadine
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a hurricane glass over fresh ice.
  4. Garnish with an orange slice and a cherry.
