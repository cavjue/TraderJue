Fog Cutter

History:

The Fog Cutter is a Trader Vic creation, first published in his 1946 Book of Food and Drink as one of his signature drinks. It mixes rum, gin, and brandy with citrus and orgeat, and finishes with a float of sherry.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ oz light rum
  • ½ oz gin
  • ½ oz brandy
  • 2 oz fresh orange juice
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz orgeat syrup
  • ½ oz cream sherry
  • Crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Add rum, gin, brandy, orange juice, lemon juice, and orgeat to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Pour into a tall glass over crushed ice.
  4. Float the sherry on top and garnish with a mint sprig.

Source:
