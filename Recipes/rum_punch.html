Rum Punch

History:

Punch is one of the oldest mixed drinks. British East India Company sailors were mixing spirit, citrus, sugar, and spice in India by the early 1600s, and the first written mention of punch dates to 1632. Rum punch became the Caribbean's own version, often made to the rhyme “one of sour, two of sweet, three of strong, four of weak.”

Ingredients:

  • 3 oz dark rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 2 oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz pineapple juice
  • 2 oz fresh orange juice
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a tall glass over fresh ice.
  4. Grate fresh nutmeg over the top and garnish with a lime wheel.
