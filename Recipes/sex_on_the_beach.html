Sex on the Beach

Ingredients:

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • ½ oz peach schnapps
  • 1½ oz fresh orange juice
  • 1½ oz cranberry juice
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a highball glass over fresh ice.
  4. Garnish with an orange slice.
