Sex on the Beach

History:

Sex on the Beach is a classic 1980s drink. It became popular after peach schnapps took off in the mid-1980s, and bartenders used it to make both the Fuzzy Navel and the Sex on the Beach. The drink was mentioned in the 1988 Tom Cruise movie Cocktail.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • ½ oz peach schnapps
  • 1½ oz fresh orange juice
  • 1½ oz cranberry juice
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a highball glass over fresh ice.
  4. Garnish with an orange slice.
