Piña Colada

History:

The Piña Colada is Puerto Rico's national drink. The Caribe Hilton in San Juan credits bartender Ramón “Monchito” Marrero with creating it there in the early 1950s, and the Barrachina restaurant in Old San Juan has a rival claim. It was made possible by Coco López cream of coconut, developed in Puerto Rico in 1948. An earlier “piña colada” from 1922 was just pineapple, rum, and lime, with no coconut.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz light rum
  • 1½ oz cream of coconut
  • 1½ oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 cup ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Pour into a hurricane glass.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry.
