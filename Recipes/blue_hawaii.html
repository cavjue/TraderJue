Blue Hawaii

Ingredients:

  • ¾ oz light rum
  • ¾ oz vodka
  • ½ oz blue curaçao
  • 3 oz pineapple juice
  • 1 oz sweet and sour mix
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a hurricane glass over fresh ice.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry.
