Blue Hawaii

History:

Harry Yee created the Blue Hawaii in 1957 at the Hawaiian Village Hotel in Waikiki, after a sales rep from Bols asked him to make a drink using their blue curaçao. It's named after the song “Blue Hawaii” from the 1937 Bing Crosby film Waikiki Wedding. The 1961 Elvis movie of the same name helped make it even more famous.

Ingredients:

  • ¾ oz light rum
  • ¾ oz vodka
  • ½ oz blue curaçao
  • 3 oz pineapple juice
  • 1 oz sweet and sour mix
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a hurricane glass over fresh ice.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry.
