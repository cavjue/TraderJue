El Presidente

Ingredients:

  • 1½ oz aged rum
  • ¾ oz blanc vermouth
  • ¼ oz orange curaçao
  • 1 tsp grenadine
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until well chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Garnish with an orange twist.
