Banana Daiquiri

History:

The Banana Daiquiri is most often traced to the Mountain Top bar on St. Thomas in the US Virgin Islands, which says it served its first one in the early 1950s using local Cruzan rum and fresh bananas. Other stories credit Hawaii or Cuba, so its true birthplace is uncertain. Either way, it is a fruity, blended descendant of the classic Daiquiri, which American engineer Jennings Cox is credited with making in Cuba around 1898.

Ingredients:

  • 1 large banana, sliced
  • 3 oz light rum
  • 2 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz triple sec
  • 2 teaspoons white sugar
  • Ice cubes

Instructions:

  1. In a blender, add all the ingredients except the ice and pulse until smooth.
  2. Add the ice and pulse highest setting until slushy
  3. Transfer into serving glasses and serve
