Classic Daiquiri

History:

The Daiquiri is named after a beach and iron-mining town in eastern Cuba. Around 1898, American mining engineer Jennings Cox is credited with mixing local white rum with lime and sugar for his team, and his diary records a recipe for it. US Navy officer Lucius Johnson is said to have brought it to Washington, D.C. around 1909, and it became one of the most famous rum drinks in the world.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz light rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake hard until well chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Garnish with a lime wheel.
