Watermelon Cosmopolitan

History:

A summery twist on the Cosmopolitan. Bartender Cheryl Cook says she created the Cosmo in the late 1980s at The Strand in Miami's South Beach, and Toby Cecchini made his famous version at the Odeon in New York in 1988. The drink became a star in the 1990s thanks to Sex and the City, and fruit versions like this one followed.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups watermelon, peeled, seeded and cubed
  • Ice cubes
  • 1 1/2 ounces vodka
  • 1 mint sprig

Instructions:

  1. In a blender, combine the watermelon and ice cubes. Blend until smooth.
  2. Through a fine strainer, pour the watermelon mixture into bowl. Discard the pulp.
  3. Fill a cocktail shaker with the watermelon mixture, ice, and vodka.
  4. Shake well and strain into chilled martini glass.
  5. Garnish with a mint sprig and serve immediately.
