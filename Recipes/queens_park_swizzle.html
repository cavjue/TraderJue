Queen's Park Swizzle

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz Demerara rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz demerara syrup
  • 8 fresh mint leaves
  • 4 dashes Angostura bitters
  • Crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Gently muddle mint and syrup in a tall glass.
  2. Add rum and lime juice, then fill with crushed ice.
  3. Swizzle until the glass frosts over.
  4. Top with more crushed ice and dash the bitters on top.
