Batida de Coco

History:

A batida, Portuguese for “shaken” or “milkshake,” is one of Brazil's favorite drinks, along with the Caipirinha. Both are made with cachaça, the sugar cane spirit Brazilians have been making for nearly 500 years. Batida de Coco, “beaten coconut,” is the creamy coconut version, made with coconut milk and sweetened condensed milk.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz cachaça
  • 2 oz coconut milk
  • 1 oz sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and frothy.
  3. Pour into a chilled glass.
  4. Garnish with toasted coconut flakes.
