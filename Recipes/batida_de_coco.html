Batida de Coco

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz cachaça
  • 2 oz coconut milk
  • 1 oz sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and frothy.
  3. Pour into a chilled glass.
  4. Garnish with toasted coconut flakes.
