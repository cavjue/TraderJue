Pineapple Cooler

History:

Coolers are tall, refreshing drinks made with a spirit, a mixer, and plenty of ice. The family goes back to the late 1800s, when the Remsen Cooler, named after a retired naval officer at New York's Union Club, first appeared in print in 1891. This pineapple and rum version is a modern tropical take on that idea.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • 2 oz pineapple juice
  • ¾ oz lime juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • Club soda
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Shake rum, pineapple juice, lime, and syrup with ice.
  2. Strain into a tall glass over fresh ice.
  3. Top with a splash of club soda.
  4. Stir gently and serve.
