Hemingway Daiquiri

History:

This drink was made for Ernest Hemingway at El Floridita in Havana, where he was a regular in the 1930s and '40s. He asked for a Daiquiri with no sugar and extra rum, which became the “Papa Doble” (a double). Bartender Antonio Meilan later added grapefruit juice and maraschino liqueur, and the drink became known as the Hemingway Special.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz fresh grapefruit juice
  • ¼ oz maraschino liqueur
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Garnish with a lime wheel.
