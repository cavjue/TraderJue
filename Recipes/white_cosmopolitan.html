White Cosmopolitan

History:

A paler variation on the Cosmopolitan that swaps the red cranberry juice for white cranberry. Bartender Cheryl Cook says she created the original Cosmo in the late 1980s at The Strand in Miami's South Beach, and Toby Cecchini made his famous version at the Odeon in New York in 1988. Its 1990s popularity, helped by Sex and the City, inspired many variations like this one.

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 ounce triple sec
  • 1 1/2 ounces vodka
  • 1/4 ounce fresh lime juice
  • 1 ounce cranberry juice
  • 1 lime slice
  • Ice cubes

Instructions:

  1. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice and palce the remining ingredients except the lime slice into the shaker.
  2. Shake well for about 30 seconds.
  3. Through a strainer, strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  4. Garnish with lime slice and serve.
