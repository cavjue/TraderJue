Pinky Gonzalez

History:

The Pinky Gonzales is Trader Vic's own tequila twist on his Mai Tai. Victor Bergeron created it in 1964 for Señor Pico, his Mexican-themed restaurant, and published it in his 1972 bartender's guide. The original simply added tequila to his prepared Mai Tai mix; this version builds it from scratch with lime, orange curaçao, orgeat, and two tequilas.

Ingredients:

  • "1 oz blanco tequila"
  • "1 oz reposado tequila"
  • "¾ oz fresh lime juice"
  • "½ oz orgeat syrup"
  • "½ oz orange curacao"
  • "1/4 oz agave nectar"
  • "Ice"

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker.
  2. Fill with ice and shake until chilled.
  3. Strain over fresh ice.
  4. Finish with a small splash of sparkling water if desired.

Source:

  • Difford's Guide: Pinky Gonzales
