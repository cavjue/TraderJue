Singapore Sling

History:

The Singapore Sling is credited to Ngiam Tong Boon, a Hainanese bartender at the Long Bar in Singapore's Raffles Hotel, sometime between 1899 and 1915. It was probably first called the Straits Sling. Nobody knows the original recipe for sure: the version Raffles serves today traces back to a note written by a visitor in 1936, and the pink, pineapple-heavy style became popular when the hotel promoted the drink again in the 1970s.

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 oz gin
  • 1/2 oz cherry liqueur
  • 1/4 oz cointreau
  • 1/4 oz benedictine
  • 2 oz pineapple juice
  • 1/2 oz lime juice
  • 1 dash angostura bitters
  • 2 oz club soda
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add the gin, cherry liqueur, Cointreau, Bénédictine, pineapple juice, lime juice, and bitters in a shaker.
  2. Shake well until cold.
  3. Strain into tall glass (highball or Collins).
  4. Fill with ice and top with club soda.
  5. Stir gently and garnish.

Source:

  • Difford's Guide: Ngiam Tong Boon and the Singapore Sling
