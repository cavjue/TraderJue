Mango & Pineapple Daiquiri

History:

This tropical blend is a modern take on the frozen Daiquiri. The original Daiquiri, rum with lime and sugar, is credited to American engineer Jennings Cox in Cuba around 1898. In the 1930s, bartender Constantino Ribalaigua at Havana's El Floridita made blended Daiquiris famous, and bars across the Caribbean have since added fruit like mango and pineapple.

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 oz mango nectar
  • 1 1/2 oz pineapple juice
  • 1 1/2 oz triple sec
  • 1 1/2 oz light rum
  • 1 lime
  • Ice cubes

Instructions:

  1. In a blender, add all the ingredients except the ice and pulse until smooth.
  2. Add the ice and pulse highest setting until slushy
  3. Transfer into serving glasses and serve
