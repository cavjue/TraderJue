Frozen Coconut Mojito

History:

A blended, tropical spin on the Cuban Mojito. The Mojito's roots may go back to “El Draque,” a 16th-century drink of cane spirit, lime, sugar, and mint, and its first known recipe appeared in a Cuban book in 1927. Adding coconut and blending it with ice is a modern beach-bar twist.

Ingredients:

  • 7 ounces cream of coconut
  • 2 1/2 ounces white rum
  • 2 1/2 limes, rind removed
  • 5 fresh mint sprigs, leaves removed
  • 4 cups ice cubes
  • 2 mint sprigs

Instructions:

  1. In a blender, combine the cream of coconut, rum, limes, mint leaves, and ice cubes. Blend until smooth.
  2. Pour into glasses and garnish with mint sprigs.
