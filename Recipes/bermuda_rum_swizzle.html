Bermuda Rum Swizzle

History:

The Rum Swizzle is another Bermuda favorite, said to have been first made at Bermuda's Swizzle Inn in the 1930s. A swizzle is named for how it's mixed: by spinning a swizzle stick between your palms in a glass of crushed ice. Most Bermudians have their own family recipe, but Gosling's Black Seal rum and tropical juices are always in it.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz black rum
  • 2 oz pineapple juice
  • 2 oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz falernum
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • Crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a tall glass.
  2. Fill with crushed ice.
  3. Swizzle until the glass frosts over.
  4. Garnish with an orange slice and a cherry.
