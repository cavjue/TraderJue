Pineapple Margarita

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 2 oz pineapple juice
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz triple sec
  • ¼ oz agave nectar
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a salt rimmed rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge.
