Pineapple Margarita

History:

A tropical twist on the classic Margarita. The Margarita's origin is disputed, but the same mix of tequila, orange liqueur, and lime appeared in London's 1937 Café Royal Cocktail Book under the name “Picador.” Adding fresh pineapple juice makes it sweeter and more tropical.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 2 oz pineapple juice
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz triple sec
  • ¼ oz agave nectar
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a salt rimmed rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge.
