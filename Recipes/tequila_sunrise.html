Tequila Sunrise

History:

An early Tequila Sunrise was served around 1930 at the Agua Caliente resort in Tijuana, Mexico, but it was a different drink, made with lime and crème de cassis. The orange juice version we know today was created around 1970 by bartender Bobby Lozoff at the Trident in Sausalito, California. The Rolling Stones drank it on their 1972 tour, which made it famous.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 4 oz fresh orange juice
  • ¼ oz grenadine
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice.
  2. Add tequila and orange juice and stir.
  3. Slowly pour the grenadine down the side so it sinks to the bottom.
  4. Garnish with an orange slice and a cherry.
