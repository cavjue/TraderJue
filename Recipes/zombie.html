Zombie

History:

Donn Beach created the Zombie in 1934 at Don the Beachcomber in Hollywood, the bar often credited with starting the tiki craze. He blended three rums with several citrus juices and syrups, and kept the exact recipe secret, even from his own bartenders. It became so strong a hit that bars limited guests to two. Beach changed the recipe over the years, and the version most people know today was pieced back together by tiki historian Jeff “Beachbum” Berry from a 1937 bartender's notebook.

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 oz Jamaican dark rum
  • 1.5 oz Puerto Rican gold rum
  • 1 oz Demerara rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz falernum
  • ½ oz Don's Mix (2 parts fresh grapefruit juice and 1 part cinnamon syrup)
  • 1 tsp grenadine syrup
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters
  • 6 drops Pernod

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake vigorously until well chilled.
  3. Strain into a tall glass over crushed ice.
  4. Stir gently and garnish.
