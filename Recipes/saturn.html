Saturn

History:

The Saturn is one of the few classic tiki drinks made with gin. It was created by José “Popo” Galsini, a Filipino-born bartender at the Palms in the Jungle restaurant in Anaheim, California. First called the X-15 after the rocket plane, it was renamed the Saturn and won an international bartending championship in the late 1960s. Jeff Berry rediscovered the recipe on a souvenir glass in a thrift shop.

Ingredients:

  • 1¼ oz gin
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz passion fruit syrup
  • ¼ oz falernum
  • ¼ oz orgeat syrup
  • 1 cup crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender with crushed ice.
  2. Flash blend for 5 seconds.
  3. Pour into a stemmed glass.
  4. Garnish with a lemon peel and cherry to look like Saturn's rings.
