Royal Hawaiian

History:

The Royal Hawaiian was created in 1948 at the Moana Hotel on Waikiki Beach and first appeared in print in Ted Saucier's 1951 book Bottoms Up. It's a gin, pineapple, lemon, and orgeat drink. It's thought to have later become the signature cocktail of the Princess Kaiulani Hotel, which opened next to the Moana in the 1950s.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ oz gin
  • 1 oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz orgeat syrup
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge.
