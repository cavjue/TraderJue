Ti' Punch

History:

Ti' Punch is the national drink of Martinique and is also popular in Guadeloupe. “Ti” is Creole for “petit,” so the name means “little punch.” It's made with rhum agricole, which is distilled from fresh sugar cane juice. The earliest known written mention is in an 1890 travel book by Lafcadio Hearn, and on the islands it's enjoyed at any time of day.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz rhum agricole blanc
  • 1 tsp cane syrup
  • 1 small disc of lime peel with a little flesh
  • Ice (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Squeeze the lime disc into a rocks glass and drop it in.
  2. Add the cane syrup.
  3. Add the rhum agricole.
  4. Stir to combine and add ice if desired.
