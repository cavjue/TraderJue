Planter's Punch

History:

Planter's Punch is a classic Jamaican rum punch from the late 1800s, often credited to Fred L. Myers, founder of Myers's rum. Jamaican punch follows an old rhyme: “one of sour, two of sweet, three of strong, four of weak.” Donn Beach later served it at Don the Beachcomber, which helped make it popular well beyond the Caribbean.

Ingredients:

  • 3 oz Jamaican dark rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 1 tsp grenadine
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters
  • Splash of club soda
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add rum, lime juice, simple syrup, grenadine, and bitters to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a tall glass over fresh ice.
  4. Top with a splash of club soda and garnish with an orange slice and mint sprig.
