Cuba Libre

History:

The Cuba Libre, “Free Cuba,” dates to around 1900, just after the Spanish–American War. The best-known story says a US Army officer in Havana ordered rum with the new American soda Coca-Cola and a lime, and the bar toasted “¡Por Cuba libre!” That story comes mainly from a 1960s Bacardi ad, so some historians doubt the details.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 4 oz cola
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice.
  2. Add rum and lime juice.
  3. Top with cola.
  4. Stir gently and garnish with a lime wedge.
