Mojito

History:

The Mojito comes from Havana, but its exact origin is unknown. Some trace it to “El Draque,” a 16th-century drink of crude cane spirit, lime, sugar, and mint named after Sir Francis Drake. The first known recipe appeared in a Cuban book in 1927, and the name Mojito was on a Havana bar menu by 1931. The popular story that Hemingway drank them at La Bodeguita del Medio has never been proven.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 8 fresh mint leaves
  • Club soda
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Gently muddle mint and simple syrup in a highball glass.
  2. Add rum and lime juice.
  3. Fill with ice and top with club soda.
  4. Stir gently and garnish with a mint sprig and lime wheel.
