Mai Tai

Ingredients:

  • 1 oz light rum
  • 1 oz dark rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 3/4 oz orange curaçao
  • 1/2 oz orgeat syrup
  • 1/4 oz simple syrup
  • Crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Pour into a rocks glass over crushed ice.
  4. Garnish with a mint sprig and lime shell.
  5. Float with 1/4 oz - 1/2 oz dark rum.
  6. Crushed ice
