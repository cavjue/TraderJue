Mai Tai


Created in the 1940s by the legendary Trader Vic, the Mai Tai is the epitome of a tiki cocktail. This hard hitting cocktail is perfect for any occassion.

If you want it to be more citrusy, leave the lime shells in the shaker while shaking.

Fun fact, the lime shell represents an island and the mint sprig represents a palm tree. Almost like you're stuck on a deserted island with nothing but this drink!

Ingredients:

  • 1 oz light rum
  • 1 oz dark rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 3/4 oz orange curaçao
  • 1/2 oz orgeat syrup
  • 1/4 oz simple syrup
  • Crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Pour into a rocks glass over crushed ice.
  4. Garnish with a mint sprig and lime shell.
  5. Float with 1/4 oz - 1/2 oz dark rum.
