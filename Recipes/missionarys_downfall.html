Missionary's Downfall

History:

Donn Beach created the Missionary's Downfall at Don the Beachcomber in Hollywood in the late 1930s. It's a refreshing frozen blend of rum, peach brandy, pineapple, honey, lime, and lots of fresh mint. Like many of Beach's drinks, it had a playful name and a recipe he kept secret.

Ingredients:

  • 1 oz white rum
  • ½ oz peach brandy
  • ½ oz honey syrup
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ cup fresh pineapple chunks
  • 10 fresh mint leaves
  • 1 cup crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Pour into a coupe or stemmed glass.
  4. Garnish with a mint sprig.
