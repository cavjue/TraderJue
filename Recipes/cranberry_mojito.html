Cranberry Mojito

History:

This is a modern, fruity twist on the classic Mojito. The Mojito comes from Cuba, and some trace it back to “El Draque,” a 16th-century drink of cane spirit, lime, sugar, and mint named after Sir Francis Drake. Its first known recipe appeared in a Cuban book in 1927, and adding fruit like cranberry is a recent variation.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 3/4 cup light rum
  • 1 1/4 cups fresh lime juice
  • 2 bottles sparkling water, chilled
  • 3/4 cup frozen cranberry juice cocktail concentrate, thawed
  • Ice cubes

Instructions:

  1. In a large pitcher, add mint leaves and sugar and muddle together until the mint is fragrant and the sugar is dissolved.
  2. Add the rum, lime juice, sparkling water, and cranberry juice concentrate to the pitcher and stir to combine.
  3. Fill glasses with ice cubes and pour the mojito mixture over the ice.
  4. Garnish with additional mint leaves and cranberries, if desired.
