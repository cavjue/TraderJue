Mary Pickford

History:

The Mary Pickford was created in Havana during Prohibition, when many American bartenders moved to Cuba for work. It's credited to bartender Fred Kaufman and named for silent film star Mary Pickford, “America's Sweetheart.” The earliest recipe, from 1928, was just pineapple juice, rum, and grenadine; maraschino liqueur was added later.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • 1½ oz pineapple juice
  • 1 tsp grenadine
  • 1 tsp maraschino liqueur
  • Ice

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Garnish with a cherry.

Source:

  • Difford's Guide: Mary Pickford
