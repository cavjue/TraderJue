Caipirinha

History:

The Caipirinha is Brazil's national cocktail, made with cachaça, a spirit distilled from sugar cane juice. Its name roughly means “little country girl,” and it is thought to come from the countryside of São Paulo state. Some stories say it started as a home remedy of cachaça, lime, and honey, but its exact origins are unknown.

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz cachaça
  • ½ lime, cut into wedges
  • 2 tsp white sugar
  • Crushed ice

Instructions:

  1. Muddle the lime wedges and sugar in a rocks glass.
  2. Fill the glass with crushed ice.
  3. Add cachaça.
  4. Stir well and serve.
