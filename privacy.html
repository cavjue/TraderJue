Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Trader Jue (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website at www.traderjue.com (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit the Site, who collects it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

The Site is a collection of cocktail recipes. There are no accounts, forms, or sign-ups, and we do not ask for or store any personal information ourselves.

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages and shows ads through Google AdSense. When you visit, these services may automatically receive information such as:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • The pages you visit and the page that referred you
  • Cookies and similar identifiers set by Google for advertising

3. Cookies and Advertising

We do not set our own cookies. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on the Site. Google’s advertising cookies enable it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and other sites on the internet, and to measure how ads perform.

Where required by law, such as for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before advertising cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time through the privacy and cookie message shown on the Site.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google Fonts does not set cookies, but your browser contacts Google’s servers to download the fonts, which shares your IP address with Google.

4. How Information Is Used

  • By Google, to show and measure ads on the Site, and to personalize ads if you allow it
  • By GitHub, to host the Site, keep it secure, and maintain server logs

We do not use this information to identify you personally.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any ourselves. Information collected by Google and GitHub is handled under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their policies.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, but no internet-based transmission is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal data. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best made to Google using the links above. You can also contact us through the repository linked below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
